FAQ

An ad doesn't play.

Ads need a network connection and are sometimes not available. Wait a moment and try again.

I bought something but don't have it.

Open the Shop in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
